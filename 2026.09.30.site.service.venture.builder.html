For founders, SMEs and corporate entrepreneurs

CBIT Venture Builder

This is for you if…

…you run a UK venture that has traction and is ready to scale, a small or medium-sized business, or a family business you are taking over, and you want to grow it;

…or you are building a new venture inside the organisation you work for.

The CBIT Venture Builder helps early-stage and growing teams build the skills and resources to scale, change direction and raise funding, with one-to-one support from a sparring partner who challenges your plans and works alongside your leaders.

0% equity · 0% profit share · £0 fees, for founders and SMEs

For corporate entrepreneurs, the terms are agreed with the company.

Building a venture inside your organisation? Email cbit.vb@ntu.ac.uk instead: the terms are agreed with the company.

What you get

  • One-to-one “sparring partner” support in four pillars: Business Modelling & Innovation Clinic, Product Roadmap & Process Design, Growth Hacking in “Blue Ocean”, and Investment Readiness Support
  • Introductions to investors and partners, and amplified media support
  • Grant writing and co-development
  • Software offers, in-house hardware, researchers and a mobile data lab
  • For SMEs: a free first step, hosting a student consultancy or technical project
  • For corporate entrepreneurs: design, validate and launch corporate ventures, using CBIT’s playbook

Format

  • Regular one-to-one sessions over 6 to 10 months
  • Student projects of 6 to 8 weeks, or one academic year

The AI edition

One-to-one support to build and grow an AI-native venture, free to founders and SMEs.

“We transform ventures using AI; all cohorts are AI-empowered or AI-native now.” Professor Xiao Ma, Director, CBIT

What AI adds

  • Proposed: work each of the four pillars with the AI methods CBIT already teaches: AI-assisted market and competitor scans (as in STEAM Founder), one process redesigned with AI support and human checks (as in AI-Powered Business Transformation), growth experiments, and an investor-ready data room.
  • Delivered: a Venture Builder cohort firm, BarberBoss, went on to the KTP that put AI into its supply chain. Funded AI projects CBIT era
  • Waiting for the registry: the Q2 2025 cohort results, including how many firms run AI in core operations.

AI-native outcome: ventures whose core processes (research, sales, operations, reporting) run as AI-supported workflows that the team owns, checks and improves.

The Venture Builder journey

The journey takes 6 to 10 months and has four distinct but connected pillars. Each pillar is flexible and runs through regular one-to-one strategy sessions, so the journey follows what your venture needs.

Pillar 1 Business Modelling & Innovation Clinic

Uncover a broad range of innovative opportunities for your business, and prioritise the ones with the greatest potential to create value.

  • Identify new opportunities and product-market fit
  • Prioritise ideas by the value they add
  • Explore and map new ideas, products and markets
  • Identify and capitalise on value propositions based on market needs
  • Redesign business models and value propositions, iteratively

Pillar 2 Product Roadmap & Process Design

Strengthen your business processes by creating new ones or improving the ones you have. Pinpoint the tasks that add value, give each an owner and set key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure success.

  • Create new business processes and improve existing ones
  • Plan value-added tasks and assign owners
  • Set short-term and long-term KPIs
  • Adopt the tools and skills to use new technology
  • Design the product roadmap

Pillar 3 Growth Hacking in “Blue Ocean”

Win new customers, get them using your product and keep them, at scale and at low cost, through quick experiments. “Blue Ocean” means markets with little or no competition. It applies whether you sell to consumers or to other businesses, or through other commercial contracts.

  • Identify key customers and segments
  • Outline key growth metrics
  • Build sales funnels and strategies that acquire and activate customers
  • Run rapid experiments to find growth-hack tactics
  • Target, onboard and retain customers at scale

Pillar 4 Investment Readiness Support

Become ready for public or private funding through tailored due diligence, co-developed bid writing and practice pitches.

  • Co-build investment collateral
  • Co-develop due-diligence intelligence
  • Identify relevant public and private funding opportunities
  • Practise pitches and build your deck
  • Get help with grant funding and consortium matchmaking

What ventures get

Every venture accepted into the CBIT Venture Builder gets access to software and hardware, select university infrastructure and expertise, and the CBIT founders network.

  • Software and support offers

    Offers from Lovable, GitHub for Startups, HubSpot, Miro, Notion, Stripe, MathWorks, MongoDB, Solid Edge for Startups, Linear, Make and SeedLegals. Co-working in Nottingham at Antenna, and product design with Jenga.

  • In-house hardware

    Kit to sign out includes a survey drone with thermal and lidar cameras, a 3D printer, cameras and point-of-sale kit.

  • Mobile data lab

    A mobile data lab that ventures can use to gather primary data.

  • People and networks

    Nottingham Business School postgraduate interns and consultants, through the Postgraduate Consultancy Project and the Technical Development Project. CBIT researchers and the CBIT founders network.

Impact story

CBIT Venture Builder

A UK air-purifier maker grew with the Venture Builder, then joined a funded project in Thailand

In their words: In a testimonial, Healthy Air says the support “enabled us to enter four new countries”.

Read the story: A UK air-purifier maker grew with the Venture Builder, then joined a funded project in Thailand

Who: Healthy Air Technology, a UK maker of air-purification products and a member of the CBIT Venture Builder cohort.

The challenge
The company was developing smart air purifiers and wanted to grow beyond the UK.
What CBIT did
CBIT supported it through the CBIT Venture Builder, CBIT’s programme of one-to-one support for founders and SMEs. In 2024 the company joined CBIT’s British Council bid with Chiang Mai University as an associated partner and “venture in residence”.
What happened
The bid won funding in 2025. Healthy Air is part of the project as a UK venture committed to exploring the Thai market with CBIT.
Where it can lead
Ventures can join CBIT’s funded projects as partners, or seek innovation funding with CBIT.
