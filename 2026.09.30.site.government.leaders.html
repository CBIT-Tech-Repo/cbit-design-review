How CBIT works with government leaders and international partners

  • National AI skills programmes

    Government-commissioned programmes, delivered with a partner university in your country: sector AI courses and hands-on workshops for industry workers, researchers, incubator managers and chief executives; or AI-Powered Business Transformation, custom-made for your own leaders.

  • AI-Empowered Going Global for delegations

    An executive programme on taking businesses into new markets, with bilingual support, for the delegations you send.

  • Build your innovation ecosystem

    Ecosystem building at national scale, with Train-the-Trainer for your science-park, technology-transfer and incubator staff.

  • Send a founder cohort to the UK

    International founder sprints: 10–14 days in the UK for the student and researcher founders you select.

  • Sister centres and long-term partnerships

    A framework memorandum of understanding and a sister centre that hosts repeat programmes, reciprocal soft landing for ventures and joint bids.

  • Land your ventures in the UK

    The Global VB Partnership: help for the start-ups and spinouts you select to register in the UK and expand into the UK and EU.

  • AI tender matching for regional SMEs (TNDRX)

    SME capability profiles matched to public tenders by AI, SME workshops and consortium building for tenders.

  • Evaluate a public programme

    STEAM Xcelerate: impact evaluation for publicly funded programmes, across economic, social and environmental measures.

  • Keynotes and masterclasses

    A keynote by Professor Xiao Ma, or a masterclass for your leaders, teams or delegations.

AI tender matching for regional SMEs, with TNDRX

Help your region’s SMEs win public contracts, with AI matching their capabilities to live tenders.

  • SME capability profiles matched to public tenders by AI.
  • SME workshops.
  • Consortium building for tenders.

TNDRX, CBIT’s platform, uses AI to profile a company’s capability, match it to live tenders and broker consortium bids, so smaller firms can bid together for contracts none could win alone.

AI-native outcome: SMEs with an AI-maintained capability profile that surfaces the tenders they can win, alone or with partners. Examples: the East Midlands construction hub and the MOSMOP and Thai MHESI programmes CBIT era.

Format

A three-month feasibility study and prototype, to start.

National AI skills programmes

A ministry-funded programme took practical AI training to businesses across Thailand

Who: Thailand’s Ministry of Higher Education, Science, Research and Innovation (MHESI), which funded the programme, and Chiang Mai University’s Science and Technology Park (STeP), which led it.

Read the full story: A ministry-funded programme took practical AI training to businesses across Thailand

The challenge
MHESI funded STeP to build AI skills among industrial workers, graduates, researchers, incubator managers and science-park officers.
What CBIT did
After a needs assessment, CBIT and STeP designed sector courses: wellness and tourism, marketing and data, food manufacturing, chief executives, and incubator managers. CBIT ran hands-on three-day workshops across Thailand in 2026, with follow-up support and joint MHESI, Chiang Mai University and Nottingham Business School certificates.
What happened
Participants began designing AI workflows and prototypes for their own organisations.
Track record
CBIT’s Thai programmes to date: 500+ professionals trained in Thailand (MHESI and Chiang Mai University programmes, 2024–2026).
Where it can lead
In April 2026 MHESI and NTU signed a five-year memorandum of understanding, with a CBIT sister centre at STeP.
