Research project

BeefTwin

An AI digital twin for beef farming

School: Nottingham Business School

Research centre: CBIT

Key facts

Funder
UKRI Cross-Research Council Responsive Mode, Round 1
Value
£1.2m
Period
February 2025 – January 2027
CBIT role
Project Lead (NBS-led)
Status
Active

Source: CBIT figures registry v1.1 (21 September 2026). Figures and sources →

About the project

BeefTwin is an AI digital twin for beef farming. It is funded by UKRI’s Cross-Research Council Responsive Mode and led by Nottingham Business School.

Aims

  • Reduce greenhouse-gas emissions per kilogram of protein
  • Improve productivity and quality
  • Improve animal welfare
  • Train smarter farmers

Contact

For enquiries about BeefTwin, email cbit@ntu.ac.uk.
