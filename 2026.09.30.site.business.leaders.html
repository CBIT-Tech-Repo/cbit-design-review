Start with AI-Powered Business Transformation, for CEOs, boards and senior teams.

For CEOs, boards and senior teams leading change; companies that want a programme of their own; owner-managers taking a business into new markets; and corporate entrepreneurs building new ventures inside their organisations.

What AI-native means for your leaders and your ventures.

How CBIT works with business leaders and companies

  • AI-Powered Business Transformation

    Not an AI tools course: business transformation, empowered by AI. For CEOs, boards and senior teams, as an open CEO cohort or a private in-company programme.

  • AI-Empowered Going Global

    The same method, Bring Your Own Case, applied to entering new markets, with bilingual support.

  • Microcredential

    Earn credit: Leading AI-Driven Business Transformation, an NTU award of 10 credits at level 7.

  • Executive PhD

    Doctoral study with your own company as the living case.

  • Build a venture inside your organisation

    The CBIT Venture Builder for corporate entrepreneurs, on terms agreed with the company.

  • Funded AI projects

    Put AI into your operations, co-funded: KTPs, Innovate UK and EU projects that bring research and new technology into your business.

  • The MSc

    For individuals: a year of week-long “Build → Measure → Learn” sprints at City Campus, Nottingham.

  • Keynotes and masterclasses

    A keynote by Professor Xiao Ma, or a masterclass for your leadership day.

Impact stories

AI-Powered Business Transformation

On a national AI programme, Thai CEOs designed AI support for a real business process

Who: Chief executives and senior managers on the CEO track of an AI skills programme funded by Thailand’s Ministry of Higher Education, Science, Research and Innovation (MHESI).

Read the full story: On a national AI programme, Thai CEOs designed AI support for a real business process

The challenge
Leaders wanted to connect AI strategy with the way their organisations actually work.
What CBIT did
CBIT designed and delivered “AI for Strategic Management for CEO” at Chiang Mai University’s Science and Technology Park: a day on AI strategy, a day turning one process into a standard operating procedure with AI support and human checks, and a day building a simple business operating system.
What happened
Participants left with first executive workflows, assistants or prototypes grounded in their own businesses.
Track record
See the track record on this page
Where it can lead
The same programme can serve a ministry’s senior team, an open CEO cohort or a single company.

Microcredential

Rolls-Royce ran an accredited CBIT course for its staff, in-company

Who: Rolls-Royce, which contracted CBIT to train its staff.

Read the full story: Rolls-Royce ran an accredited CBIT course for its staff, in-company

The challenge
The company wanted accredited training on disruptive technology for its staff.
What CBIT did
CBIT delivered its microcredential, Leading AI-Driven Business Transformation, in-company, with commercialisation workshops. The course is an NTU award of 10 credits at level 7, which stacks towards the PG Cert in Leadership and Management Practice.
What happened
CBIT completed delivery to Rolls-Royce and has since adapted the course for executive cohorts in Thailand and China.
Where it can lead
Further in-company cohorts can follow, or credit towards a postgraduate certificate.
