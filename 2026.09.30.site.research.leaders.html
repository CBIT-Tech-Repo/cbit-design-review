Research and university leaders

Win interdisciplinary research, and turn it into ventures and impact.

Email us about your bid Help me choose

Start with STEAM Xcelerate: interdisciplinary by design, impact by default.

For principal investigators and research directors; pro-vice-chancellors for research and enterprise, deans, and heads of technology-transfer offices (TTOs), commercialisation teams and science parks, in the UK and overseas.

What makes a person AI-native

How CBIT works with research and university leaders

  • Interdisciplinary research, from bid to impact

    STEAM Xcelerate: the method behind CBIT’s two awards in UKRI’s Cross-Research Council Responsive Mode, brought into your next bid, then impact tracked and analysed by AI agents with ImpactTracker, CBIT’s platform.

  • Venture partner for your bid

    A named partner that runs the venture-creation or commercialisation work in your bid, with Venture Builder support as in-kind match.

  • A programme for your researchers

    STEAM Founder cohorts that turn research into ventures: an AI-first lean start-up journey for researchers, technicians and student founders.

  • Executive PhD routes for your CDT or DTC bid

    Doctoral routes inside your doctoral-training bid: the Executive PhD route and the practitioner-doctorate route.

  • Build your innovation ecosystem

    Build or strengthen a research-commercialisation ecosystem, with Train-the-Trainer for your technology-transfer, science-park and incubator staff.

  • Founder sprints

    A UK sprint for the student and researcher founders you send: market validation, mentoring, a showcase and a pitch day.

  • Sister centres

    A long-term partnership: a sister centre that hosts repeat programmes, reciprocal soft landing for ventures and joint bids.

  • Keynotes and masterclasses

    A keynote by Professor Xiao Ma, or a masterclass for your leaders, teams or researchers.

Interdisciplinary research bids

Many disciplines, one method: UKRI’s panel graded the BeefTwin bid A*

In the funder’s words: UKRI’s panel called it an “outstanding proposal which has very strong potential to result in key and transformative change”.

Who: BeefTwin, a UKRI-funded consortium of universities, farms and industry, led by CBIT at Nottingham Trent University.

Read the full story: Many disciplines, one method: UKRI’s panel graded the BeefTwin bid A*

The challenge
Feed, farming and food pull on income, welfare and emissions at once, a triple-bottom-line problem no single discipline can balance.
What CBIT did
CBIT built the bid on its Sparring Partnership method, where disciplines challenge each other’s ideas, and on lean start-up sprints of build, measure and learn.
What happened
UKRI funded the bid. Professor Xiao Ma, CBIT’s Director, led it.

Work with CBIT

You contract with Nottingham Trent University (NTU). CBIT, the Centre for Business and Industry Transformation, is part of Nottingham Business School at NTU. NTU signs grants, contracts and agreements; CBIT delivers the work. The CBIT Venture Builder is CBIT’s programme for founders.

Start small: bring us into one bid at the expression-of-interest stage, for its interdisciplinary method or its venture engine, or run one STEAM Founder cohort for your researchers.
