Figures and sources

Every figure on this website, where it comes from and the pages that use it. Each is quoted in one wording, with its period.

How we quote figures

  • Each figure is quoted in the registry’s own wording, with its period or as-at date.
  • CBIT’s figures and Professor Xiao Ma’s career figures are labelled, so the two are never confused.
  • A figure that is not in the registry does not go on the website until it has been added to the registry.

The registry: CBIT figures registry v1.3, 27 September 2026, owned by Professor Xiao Ma, Director, CBIT.
