Keynotes and masterclasses

This is for you if…

…you organise a conference or summit, plan a leadership day for your company, lead a research team, host a government delegation, or run a trade body.

A keynote or a masterclass from CBIT’s team, for leaders, teams or researchers, drawing on CBIT’s programmes: AI-powered business transformation, venture building, research commercialisation and interdisciplinary research.

Next step

Tell us the audience, the theme and the date, and which speaker you have in mind, or ask us to suggest one.

Email us about a keynote or masterclass

If your email app does not open, write to cbit@ntu.ac.uk

What you get

  • A keynote from one of CBIT’s speakers for your conference, summit or leadership day.
  • A masterclass for your leaders, teams or researchers.
  • Themes from CBIT’s programmes: AI-powered business transformation, venture building, research commercialisation and interdisciplinary research.

A conference keynote

Keynotes and masterclasses

Professor Xiao Ma closed CBIT’s own conference on AI for smaller businesses

Read the story: Professor Xiao Ma closed CBIT’s own conference on AI for smaller businesses

Who: Owners and leaders of small and medium-sized businesses, at the Innovate UK AI for SME Conference in Nottingham.

The challenge
Smaller businesses want practical, affordable ways to put AI to work, not just big-company case studies.
What CBIT did
On 20 November 2025, Professor Xiao Ma gave the closing keynote, “The Road Ahead”, at Transforming SMEs with AI, the Innovate UK AI for SME Conference that CBIT co-hosted with Innovate UK Business Connect.
What happened
The conference’s practical AI-adoption stories, sessions and resources continue at ai4smes.co.uk, hosted by CBIT and supported by Innovate UK.
Track record
CBIT runs Nottingham Global Entrepreneurship Week — the only GEW activity in the East Midlands — and co-hosts the annual Innovate UK AI for SME Conference.
Where it can lead
A first step: invite Professor Xiao Ma to your own conference, summit or team.

ai4smes.co.uk, hosted by CBIT, supported by Innovate UK
